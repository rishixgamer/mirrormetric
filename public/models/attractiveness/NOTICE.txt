No SCUT-derived preference-model pack is distributed in this build.

MirrorMetric source and training code are MIT licensed. SCUT-FBP5500 and any
derived model pack remain subject to separate dataset terms. A public pack may
be added only after redistribution rights are confirmed, all benchmark gates
pass, and its exact bytes are pinned by VITE_ATTRACTIVENESS_MODEL_SHA256.

Without that pack, MirrorMetric uses its MIT-licensed, project-defined geometry
balance fallback. The fallback is not trained on attractiveness labels and is
not presented as a validated SCUT estimate.
